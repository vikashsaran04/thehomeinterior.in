Our Work Process

A Clear 5-Step Journey to Your New Home

From initial discussion to final keys, here is how we systematically take your interior project forward.

1

Consultation

We discuss your flat layout, family requirements, lifestyle preferences, and tentative budget.

2

Site Visit

Our team visits your home in Chennai to take laser-accurate site measurements and examine structural walls.

3

Design & Planning

We finalize 2D/3D layouts, color schemes, modular cabinet internals, and exact material selections.

4

Execution

Skilled craftsmen execute civil modifications, false ceiling, carpentry, electrical, and painting with regular site reviews.

5

Final Handover

Thorough quality check of all hinges, drawers, surface polishing, and thorough cleaning before key handover.
