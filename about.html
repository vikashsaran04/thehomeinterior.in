About Naveen Interior Studio

Dedicated to Honest Interior Craftsmanship in Chennai

Started in 2026, Naveen Interior Studio brings practical design, dependable materials, and single-window turnkey execution to homeowners across Chennai.

Our Story

How Naveen Interior Studio Began

Founded in 2026 in Pammal, Chennai, Naveen Interior Studio was established to resolve the most common challenge homeowners face: fragmented contractors and poor on-site coordination.

Most apartment buyers find themselves negotiating separately with carpenters, false ceiling teams, painters, and electricians. This often leads to conflicting schedules, finger-pointing when measurements don't align, and rising costs.

Naveen Interior Studio eliminates this friction by bringing end-to-end turnkey interior execution under one accountable roof. We treat every home as a personal living sanctuary that must balance ergonomic space usage with long-lasting structural durability.

Our Guiding Mission

To deliver trustworthy, transparent, and aesthetically refined residential interiors across Chennai that stand the test of time and provide genuine everyday comfort.

Design Philosophy

Practicality First. Beauty Built to Last.

Our design approach is rooted in understanding how Chennai families actually use their living spaces day in and day out.

01

Climate-Resilient Material Selection

Chennai's humidity and coastal air require Boiling Water Proof (BWP) plywood for wet zones and calibrated ply for wardrobes. We never cut corners on substrate core materials.

02

Clean Lines & Clutter-Free Storage

We emphasize smart vertical integration: full-height lofts, concealed shoe racks, and modular drawer organizers that keep counter surfaces pristine and easy to clean.

03

Airflow & Natural Daylight

Our layout plans respect the natural airflow channels of your apartment, ensuring living rooms and bedrooms remain cool, airy, and naturally illuminated.

Structured Execution

Our 5-Step Turnkey Process

We follow a disciplined step-by-step workflow to ensure transparency and timely handover.

1

Consultation

Detailed discussion on floor plan, configuration (2 BHK, 3 BHK, 4 BHK), and family needs.

2

Site Visit

Laser site measurement at your apartment in Chennai and structural assessment.

3

Design & Planning

Material selection, color schemes, modular layouts, and comprehensive cost estimate.

4

Execution

Carpentry, false ceiling, electrical wiring, and painting supervised on-site.

5

Final Handover

Multi-point quality inspection, deep cleaning, and key handover to homeowner.
